# Results

Detailed write-ups of selected experiments. Every experiment, including the negative ones, has an entry in the [public ledger](../EXPERIMENTS.md). A result gets its own page here when it changes what the program believes or does next.

| Result | Date | Outcome | One line |
|---|---|---|---|
| [EXP-0044](EXP-0044.md) | 2026-10-20 | SUPPORTED | Three seeded lineages each finished two gated generations above their start: +8.2, +6.8 and +6.5 points on 600 held-out tasks, with no regression on the whole territory beyond base noise and measured forgetting below 0.01 |

## How a result page is structured

1. Header table: ID, outcome, dates, model, lineages, task counts.
2. An auditability note: what a reader can and cannot verify.
3. Question and hypothesis, as pre-registered.
4. Protocol at a conceptual level, with what is withheld stated plainly.
5. Results with significance, noise, regression and forgetting.
6. Relation to the previous experiment, interpretation, then limitations.
7. Next experiments.

Numbers on these pages are copied from the author-maintained private ledger. The ledger, raw results, task sets and training procedure are private and cannot be audited from this repository.
