# EXP-0044: Gated multi-round growth

| | |
|---|---|
| **ID** | EXP-0044 |
| **Outcome** | SUPPORTED: the pre-registered decision rule passed for 3 of 3 seeded lineages |
| **Pre-registered** | 2026-10-20 (UTC) |
| **Ran** | 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-21 (UTC) |
| **Model** | One Qwen-family open-weight model (35B-class) |
| **Lineages** | 3, labelled A, B and C, differing in training seed |
| **Final test** | 600 held-out tasks from the trained territory |

> **Auditability.** The public repository does not permit independent reproduction or audit of the private task sets, training procedure or pre-registration chronology. Numbers below are copied from the author-maintained private ledger. What can be checked from this page: the p-values recompute from the published gained and lost task counts.

## Question

Can roots grow for two rounds, keeping a change only when it beats the current root?

A **root** is a small neural adapter that gives the model a skill on one **territory**: an internally generated family of programming tasks with automatic correctness checks. A **lineage** is one root followed through its accepted successors. G0 is the root at the start of EXP-0044, G1 the root after the first accepted round, G2 after the second.

## Hypothesis

If every round must beat the current root on held-out tasks before its candidate is kept, a root can finish two rounds above its starting point, without the aggregate regression seen in EXP-0043.

## Protocol (conceptual)

Each round produced candidate updates from verified experience. A protected evaluation accepted or rejected them: a candidate replaced the current version only if it beat it on held-out tasks and passed a forgetting check. After two rounds, each lineage's G0 and G2 were scored on the same 600 held-out territory tasks in the same session, alongside the base model and a repeat run of the base model.

Growth-data selection, routing, candidate construction, training, task and verifier construction, and decision thresholds are withheld.

## Results

### Final test: 600 held-out territory tasks (base model: 340 passes)

| Lineage | G0 | G2 | Change | Tasks gained / lost (paired) | One-sided exact McNemar p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 486 | 535 | **+8.17 pts** | 82 / 33 | 2.8e-6 |
| B | 521 | 562 | **+6.83 pts** | 69 / 28 | 1.9e-5 |
| C | 489 | 528 | **+6.50 pts** | 56 / 17 | 2.6e-6 |

Against the base model, G2 is +32.5, +37.0 and +31.3 points on the territory (G0 was +24.3, +30.2 and +24.8). One repeat run of the base model changed the net territory score by -1.17 points. That is a single repeat, not a variance estimate across many runs.

### Each accepted round (held-out check, gain over the current root)

| Lineage | Round 1 | Round 2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| A | +15 | +7 |
| B | +15 | +4 |
| C | +8 | +9 |

Each round's check used a different set of held-out tasks, so these gains are not one continuous curve, and G1 was not scored on the final test. That is why the chart shows G0 and G2 on the final test and the round gains separately.

### Pre-registered gates

| Gate | Result |
|---|---|
| Final root beats starting root | Passed, 3 of 3 |
| No regression on the whole territory | Passed, 3 of 3 |
| Coverage check | Passed, 3 of 3 |
| Forgetting below 0.01 for every accepted candidate | Passed, 3 of 3 |
| A candidate accepted in every round | Yes, 6 of 6 rounds |

### Details that qualify the result

- **Forgetting was nonzero.** The forgetting metric was measured for every accepted candidate and stayed below 0.01. It does not rule out forgetting outside the measured probes. In one round, a candidate was rejected because it did not pass the forgetting check.
- **Two difficulty levels dipped.** Inside the aggregate gains, one lineage lost 7 tasks on one difficulty level and another lost 9 on another level. The no-regression gate was pre-registered on the whole territory.
- **Gains shrank in two of three lineages.** Round gains fell from +15 to +7 and from +15 to +4, and rose from +8 to +9. The supply of new learning material also fell for two of three lineages.
- **Different starting points.** Lineage A started from a root trained in EXP-0041. Lineages B and C started from roots already grown once in EXP-0043.

## Relation to EXP-0043

EXP-0043 tested one growth round. Its pre-registered decision was negative: one round of growth from a root's own verified successes did not beat more training on old data, and only 1 of 3 seeds passed every gate, because the strongest starting root regressed. EXP-0044 required each round's candidate to beat the current root before replacing it, and ran two rounds. No lineage finished below its starting point on the aggregate territory score.

## Interpretation

Under the EXP-0044 pipeline, all three seeded lineages finished above their starting roots on a separate 600-task test, and no lineage repeated EXP-0043's aggregate regression. Because EXP-0044 did not include a matched ungated or old-data control, it does not identify which component of the pipeline caused the improvement. This is single-model evidence that a skill can keep improving across two successive gated generations inside its territory.

## Limitations

- **Cause unidentified.** No ungated or equal-compute control was run in this experiment.
- **Transfer unmeasured.** EXP-0044 did not measure performance outside the trained territory. EXP-0041 found that a comparable skill did not transfer to unseen task families.
- **Seeds, not independent replications.** The three lineages share the base model, task territory, evaluation set and pipeline.
- **Not evidence of better breeders.** Two rounds do not show that later generations are better at producing improvements.
- **Two rounds only.** Whether gains continue over more rounds is open.
- **One model.** Every root is an adapter bound to one Qwen-family model. Regrowing it on another model has not been tested.
- **Not recursive self-improvement.** The improvement process itself did not change. Ouroboros does not claim unrestricted or indefinite self-improvement.

## Next experiments

1. **Transfer:** do improved roots beat their ancestors outside their own territories?
2. **Breeding:** does an improved generation have a higher chance of producing a better descendant?
3. **More rounds:** do gains continue or stop?
4. **Model independence:** EXP-0045, one mind on two model families, has since run: supported in a first small test ([results/EXP-0045.md](EXP-0045.md)).
