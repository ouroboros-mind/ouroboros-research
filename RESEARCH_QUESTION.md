# The research question

## Central question

> Can a persistent, model-independent artificial mind accumulate verified experience and progressively compile it into increasingly efficient and general forms of intelligence, while preserving identity across replaceable neural substrates and eventually improving the mechanisms by which it learns?

In plain words: can one AI grow up instead of being replaced? This is the question, not a finding. Model independence and lifelong operation have not been demonstrated.

## Moonshot

> Can this process eventually produce a personal intelligence that runs on hardware its owner controls, with frontier-like effective capability, and that keeps becoming more capable throughout its lifetime?

The moonshot sets direction. It is not a claim, and no result on this site should be read as progress toward it unless the ledger says so.

## What would count as success

The program is organized around five proofs. Each needs its own pre-registered experiment with controls. None is claimed until that experiment passes. Test designs and thresholds are withheld.

| Proof | Claim | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Cortex independence | One mind runs on two different model families and keeps its identity, memories, goals, world and self models, and skills | In progress (EXP-0045 has not run) |
| 2. Neural learning without corruption | An open-weight model learns a verified, generalized capability in its weights without corrupting the mind's other records | Not started |
| 3. Brain migration | A mature mind moves to a new model and keeps measurable continuity | Not started |
| 4. Mature beats fresh | A mature instance substantially beats its frozen seed on new tasks | Early single-model evidence inside narrow territories (EXP-0041 to EXP-0044); transfer not shown |
| 5. Recursive improvement | An improved learning process helps create another successful generation | Not started |

## Open questions

1. **Transfer.** Do skills grown inside a territory help outside it? Current evidence points the other way (EXP-0041).
2. **Better breeders.** Are later generations better at producing successful successors?
3. **Continuity across models.** What survives a model swap, in behavior and capability rather than files alone? Prior work has shown mechanical continuity without behavioral equivalence ([PRIOR_ART.md](PRIOR_ART.md#enoch-runtime-independent-persistent-agents)).
4. **Compression.** How much of a mature mind's capability can be distilled onto hardware an individual owns? No fixed memory target is set.
5. **Stable world and self models.** Do they stay calibrated when the model underneath changes?

## A separate question, with separate rules

> Can computational properties linked to selfhood and to theories of consciousness be built and causally tested, without mistaking convincing behavior for proof of sentience?

This is research on measurable mechanisms only. Ouroboros is not sentient and does not claim to be. See [docs/sentience-research.md](docs/sentience-research.md).
