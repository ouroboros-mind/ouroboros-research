# Sentience research

**Ouroboros is not sentient, and we do not claim it is.**

The project has a separate research question, with separate evidence rules:

> Can computational properties linked to selfhood and to theories of consciousness be built and causally tested, without mistaking convincing behavior for proof of sentience?

## What may be investigated

Properties that theories of consciousness associate with conscious processing, studied as mechanisms:

- a persistent self-model;
- autobiographical continuity over time;
- a global workspace whose contents are broadcast to many processes;
- recurrent integration rather than a single pass;
- metacognition: monitoring and reporting on its own states and confidence;
- an attention schema: a model of its own attention;
- interoception-like signals about its own resources and condition;
- endogenous cognition: activity not directly prompted by input;
- a distinction between the world and itself.

These are measurable computational properties. Their presence would not by itself establish phenomenal consciousness.

## Evidence rules

- **Mechanisms, not impressions.** A system saying it has feelings is not evidence. Fluent self-description is exactly what language models are good at producing.
- **Causal tests.** A property counts only if removing or disabling it changes behavior in the predicted way (lesion and ablation studies).
- **Pre-registered and recorded,** like every other experiment, with null results kept.
- **No inference to experience.** Whether any system has subjective experience remains unknown, and nothing in this track is presented as an answer to that question.

This follows the indicator-property approach of Butlin et al. and related work ([PRIOR_ART.md](../PRIOR_ART.md#consciousness-indicator-research)).

## Status

Not started. Some building blocks (a self-model, a bounded workspace, an autobiographical record) exist for engineering reasons in Cognitive Kernel v0. No sentience-track experiment has been registered.
