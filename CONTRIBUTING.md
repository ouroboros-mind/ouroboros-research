# Contributing

Thank you for your interest. This repository is the public research record for Ouroboros. The core implementation is private.

## Welcome

- **Discussion** of results, methods and limitations, in GitHub Discussions.
- **Replication reports.** If you test a claim here independently, even partially or with a different setup, please file a replication report. Negative replications are as welcome as positive ones.
- **Benchmark suggestions,** especially held-out evaluations that would test transfer, regression or forgetting.
- **Prior-art references.** If we have missed or misdescribed related work, tell us. We would rather be corrected than be wrong in public.
- **Documentation fixes** for errors, unclear wording or broken links.

Use the issue templates for each of these. Product feature requests are out of scope.

## Not accepted

- **Code or design contributions to the Ouroboros core.** The implementation is private and does not accept outside contributions.
- **Confidential or proprietary material.** Do not submit anything you do not have the right to share, including your employer's confidential information, unpublished third-party work, private datasets or credentials.

## Before you post

- Keep discussion about the research. Personal attacks and harassment are not tolerated.
- Submissions may be quoted or summarized in this repository with credit, unless you ask otherwise.
- Do not post security vulnerabilities publicly; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
